Features

Everything a print library should do

From the moment you point meshNest at a folder, it scans, understands and indexes your models — with your choice of AI provider.

🔍

Semantic search

Describe what you want — "spooky halloween ghosts decoration" — and get the closest models ranked by vector similarity, powered by embeddings.

🧠

AI descriptions & tags

Vision models describe each model in natural language with tags, print-use suggestions and confidence — stored locally in SQLite.

🖼️

Deterministic 3D previews

Three.js renders consistent multi-view thumbnails (back, front, left, perspective) for every model, even inside 3MF archives.

🌐

World search

Out of ideas? Search public 3D model sites from the app and open results with your slicer in one click.

⚙️

Provider-agnostic

Embeddings and vision via Ollama, OpenAI, Gemini or the built-in local model. Switching providers won't lose your metadata.

🔒

Local-first storage

SQLite (WAL) with the sqlite-vec extension keeps models, metadata and embedding vectors entirely on your machine.

How it works

Point, scan, done

1

Select folder

Point meshNest at any local folder — it discovers STL and 3MF files and watches for new ones.

2

Scan & index

Each file gets 3D renders extracted, 3MF internals parsed and geometry metadata stored.

3

AI describes

Your vision provider turns renders into descriptions and searchable tags. Embeddings go into a vector index.

4

Ask your library

Type what you're looking for. meshNest ranks matches, shows previews and opens the file in your slicer.

Under the hood

Built local-first

LayerStack
UIIonic 8 + Vue 3 + TypeScript, Electron shell
3Dthree.js — STL/3MF loaders, deterministic renderer
Storagebetter-sqlite3 (WAL) + sqlite-vec KNN vector search
AIOllama, OpenAI, Gemini, or local built-in (transformers.js)
Parsingfflate + fast-xml-parser for 3MF internals, zod for AI output validation
